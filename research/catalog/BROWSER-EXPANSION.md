# Browser-first expansion — 10 September 2026

The browser-focused research now adds **33 entries and 66 candidate building blocks**: the original 30-entry repository pass contributes 60 source-inspected blocks, and three official OpenAI Sites showcases contribute six official-description leads because no public source is linked. The whole research index now has **76 entries and 153 building blocks**. Separate monorepo games and model variants retain separate subproject paths; these counts do not represent 76 unrelated repositories or completed integrations.

**25 additions have official or source-level Three.js evidence**, including Void Explorer's described WebGPU/WebGL architecture. The seven repository entries outside Three.js use Canvas 2D (four), Babylon.js (one), JavaScript/WebGL (one) and React (one). Hollowflux stays classified only as browser/TypeScript because the official sources inspected do not establish its renderer. Browser availability does not guarantee mobile support or compatibility with a target game. The LAAS world, two flight simulators, six PhiloLabs world/cinematic studies and physics toolkit retain those content types.

## Rights and useful starting points

Among the new entries, 11 have inspected MIT code licenses, one GPL-3.0-only, one explicitly scoped CC0 grant, six a custom noncommercial source-available license, and ten no established project-code license. **None is extraction- or integration-tested.** Every represented reuse scope still requires review.

Toy2Game is explicitly source-available with noncommercial restrictions, not OSI open source. A collection's CC0 does not automatically clear contributed games: Thunderfall explicitly adopts it, while other contributed game scopes remain unresolved. PhiloLabs' MIT code coexists with geodata, brand and fictional-scene caveats. A model label grants no code, image or asset rights.

**69 catalog records now have authentic, record-specific media files.** The dashboard projects only 40 independently approved images. It withholds 26 media-audit candidates and the three official OpenAI images pending independent decisions, plus seven records that lack a defensible stable exact-record image. Each file retains its source, hash, dimensions, credit, rights evidence and narrow allowed-use statement in `media-manifest.json`; the contact sheet provides a visual review surface. The three OpenAI article images are labeled `LicenseRef-OpenAI-Official-Image-Rights-Unresolved`: official publication establishes identity and provenance, while the user's direction scopes them to this local dashboard and does not establish public redistribution or reuse rights. The other decisions likewise do not clear whole game asset sets, public publication, source extraction or in-game reuse.
