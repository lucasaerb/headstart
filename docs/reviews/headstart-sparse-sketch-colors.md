## Findings and aesthetic critique

**PASS — meaningful reduction in clutter.** Each new image has one connected castle headland, a tiny distant island silhouette and broad open sea/sky. The castle reads as one destination. Depth comes from coastline, path and horizon instead of repeated islands, floating masses, villages and waterfalls. This resolves the main complaint at the composition level.

**PASS — wide feeling with honest scale limits.** The world remains expansive and the castle is modest relative to the canvas. The companion is less dominant than C9 but larger than C10's tiny helper. The final notes explicitly acknowledge this difference, rather than claiming an exact C10 camera scale. The headline, prompt, browse route and bag remain clearly readable and are not shrunk with the artwork.

**PASS — useful palette choices.** C11's aqua/coral scene and teal controls give the freshest separation of product action from landscape. C12's forest-green action, muted sea and golden grass are cohesive and calm, with a more traditional illustrated-book mood. C13 carries plum consistently through type, action, cape and path cues against violet water and peach light; it is the dreamiest option. Each preserves the engraved/ink style and recognizable helper while offering a distinct color mood.

Reviewer recommendation: **C11 is the strongest next direction for product clarity and welcoming energy.** C13 is a credible alternative for a softer imaginative identity; C12 is tasteful but comparatively subdued. These are qualitative design judgments from actual images, not measured usability results or a user selection.

Nonblocking refinement: foreground grass, rocks and hatching remain dense around the helper and pause region. Reduce some of that detail in a later refinement; preserve the companion silhouette and path rather than increasing their size. C12's leafy upper-right corner adds more visual weight than the other versions. None currently obscures the main controls or defeats the requested reduction in island clutter.

**PASS — accurate documentation and scope.** Final notes describe actual teal/forest-green/plum actions, the retained horizon island and the helper-scale difference. They include an evidence-based aesthetic recommendation and clearly separate it from measured results. Image footers identify static concepts with no video/motion; the notes identify generated flat stills without separate layers or functioning controls. No catalog, source reuse, connection, royalty or performance success is claimed.

Review rounds: preliminary direct visual review identified prose alignment needs (actual action colors and helper scale). Author resolved them; final independent read verified those changes. No blocking image findings; no regeneration required.

Final verdict: **PASS** for three provisional sparse sketch/color concepts and their notes/prompts. This accepts the exploratory deliverable only. Baseline selection, mobile adaptation, accessibility behavior, layered assets and working motion remain future work.
