## What this draft establishes

The selected visual direction and desired sequence are concrete. The supplied images establish composition and intended controls only. This authoring task establishes no live MCP bag retrieval, source integration, playable remix, automatic royalty service or produced video. Each later success claim needs the corresponding evidence in the table.

Keep source/rights inspection at the selection and agent stages; unresolved rights block source-content reuse/export. Catalog/bag reads remain separate from authorized local integration. Required notices are preserved. Royalty logging is part of the target; payment/settlement and the economic terms remain separate and unspecified.

**Recommendation:** spend the longest uninterrupted interval on the playable payoff. Use one clear remix idea throughout, preserve the visible three-item bag into Astra, and keep the royalty record concise. The audience should remember what was made and where its pieces came from.
