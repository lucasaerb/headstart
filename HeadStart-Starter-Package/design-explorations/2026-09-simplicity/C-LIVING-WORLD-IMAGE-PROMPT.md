# C living-world prompt

Built-in image generation; original C board used as a visual reference. Static concept, not implemented motion.

Use case: ui-mockup. Create a NEW high-fidelity HeadStart landing page concept, an expressive evolution of visual direction C in the attached reference. The user likes C's clarity and friendly blue-white palette, but asks it to feel like landing IN A GAME: living layered landscape, little character that follows cursor, castles, birds, depth, creative AI feeling. This is an illustrative STATIC DESIGN of intended interactions. It must feel like an original playful product, not an ordinary SaaS split-hero with an image in a rounded rectangle. KEEP C's readable dark navy humanist typography and saturated blue actions, simple prompt-first purpose, no crowded dashboard. Render one large desktop browser viewport, straight-on, full-bleed, generous 3:2 landscape. Highest available resolution. Do NOT make a storyboard collage or devices on this image.

DESIGN: A miniature handcrafted 3D videogame world forms the page itself. Pale blue open sky across top and left provides clean negative space for copy. On the right midground, a magical but tasteful ivory-and-blue castle sits on grassy cliffs with terracotta roofs, small medieval village, arched bridges, tall trees, ribbons of turquoise waterfalls. Terrain curves across the LOWER THIRD beneath the content rather than being boxed into a picture. In the far distance multiple atmospheric island silhouettes, tiny flock of birds, big soft clouds; foreground a few softly out-of-focus grass blades, flowers and overhanging leaves at edges. Obvious depth strata: soft sky > distant islands > detailed castle world > foreground meadow/path > nearby plants. Layered lighting, ambient shadows, subtle volumetric light, warm sunshine, charming tactile 3D materials, original indie game craft, not flat clipart, not generic fantasy concept art, not overwhelming photorealism. Small discoveries: tiny lighthouse on distant island, windmill, two birds on a roof. Keep density controlled: strong art on RIGHT and BOTTOM, content on LEFT against CLEAN LIGHT SKY. A narrow winding walkable stone path passes through foreground meadow. A lovable tiny ORIGINAL game companion, cream rounded creature wearing a blue short cape and small tan backpack, stands on that path near bottom-center, facing a visible white arrow cursor with blue rim. A few soft blue stepping-stone dots on ground between companion and cursor demonstrate following without big arrows. Companion is small, real inhabitant of world, not a giant mascot or chatbot avatar. A small unobtrusive speech bubble near it says exactly "Let's find your starting point." No licensed character designs.

UI: transparent or near-white airy top navigation, wordmark "HeadStart" top-left, "Games" and "How it works" center/right, outlined bag icon plus "Bag 0" at far right. No browser address bar. Left content begins below nav and stays within clean light sky occupying about 44% width. Big beautifully typeset dark navy heading exactly "Every great game starts somewhere." at 2-3 lines. Under it smaller exact line "Find open-source games you love. Remix them into something yours." Then a white subtly translucent compact prompt surface with a LIGHT border and barely soft shadow, not glassy heavy backdrop. Visible label "What are you making?" and one roomy text input placeholder "A tiny city builder with a racing twist..." Blue primary button "Find my starting point". Beneath it underlined text secondary link "Or browse all games". Do not add runtime dropdown or starter/part choices on landing; user wants simplicity and library handles those. Keep search surface fully legible and unobstructed. Primary button must not overlap landscape, cursor, speech bubble or companion. At bottom-left minimal pill control with pause icon text "Pause world", and discreet text "Move your cursor to explore". Small legible bottom-right label "Interactive world concept". The bag and browse path must be obvious. No AI sparkle icons, gradient text, chatbot messages, purple neon glow, floating marketing metric cards, pricing, technical status badges, fake connected/installed states, heavy app panels or feature list. At very bottom outside viewport in thin white presentation strip: "C / A living starting point · Static mockup · Generated illustrative world · Motion not implemented". Full composition must feel alive while shown in a still image. Preserve originality and contrast; no text rendered over busy ground or water.
