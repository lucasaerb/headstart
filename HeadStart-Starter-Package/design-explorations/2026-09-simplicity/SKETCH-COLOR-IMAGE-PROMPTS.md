Across the entire sea, at most ONE small distant island silhouette near horizon, NO other islands, floating chunks, rock stacks, lighthouses, secondary castles, scattered villages, tiny islets or archipelagos. The sense of a large world comes from EMPTY DISTANCE, atmospheric value shifts and low horizon, NOT extra objects.

Preserve editorial drawn sophistication of C9 but make it much lighter, less visually noisy and more spacious. NOT photoreal 3D, clay, papercraft, pixels or cartoon vector. Continuous grounded peninsula, modest castle with 3 readable towers occupying roughly 15-20% image width in upper-right/middle-right, much smaller than C9; maybe one arched coastal bridge as part of connected peninsula. No huge stacked impossible castle complex. Foreground path starts at bottom and curves toward castle in distance.
