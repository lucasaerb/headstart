# More room for the idea

Sketch and color exploration · 10 September 2026 · provisional stills, directly inspected

Combine [C9's engraved editorial character](C9-editorial-dreamscape.png) with [C10's wider view](C10-wide-world.png), then simplify the landscape. These alternatives compare color and composition; none is an implementation baseline.

## What got quieter

C9's floating islands, clouds, waterfalls and dense engraving create competing focal points. C10 opens the view but fills it with more islands and landmarks. Pulling back alone did not reduce the visual clutter.

All three new images use **one main castle coastline and one small distant island silhouette**. Depth comes from the foreground path, sloping headland and open horizon. Large areas of sea and sky give the headline and prompt breathing room. The castle now reads as one destination rather than one among many attractions.

The companion remains easy to find without dominating the scene. It is larger than C10's tiny helper, so these retain a wider composition rather than its exact camera scale. The headline, prompt, **Browse all games** and bag remain at readable interface size.

## The three palettes

**[C11 — Lagoon and coral](C11-lagoon-coral.png).** Turquoise water, a coral sun, warm paper and a deep teal action create the freshest, clearest separation between landscape and controls. The teal cape and path cues tie the helper to the interface without making it a second large focal point. The coral remains an accent rather than competing with the primary action.

**[C12 — Sage and gold](C12-sage-gold.png).** Muted water, golden grass, cream paper and a deep forest-green action feel calm and established. This is closest to a traditional illustrated book: tasteful, cohesive, but less immediately playful. The large leafy corner and dark foreground add more visual weight than C11's open upper-right area.

**[C13 — Plum and peach](C13-plum-peach.png).** Violet water, plum ink and a warm peach sky create the dreamiest version. The purple action and companion cape are coherent, and the broad sky makes the castle feel distant. Its softer overall color separation feels more atmospheric than energetic; it could suit a more imaginative, story-led identity.

## My recommendation

**Develop C11 first.** It best balances the distinctive sketch treatment with the practical clarity of a product landing page. C13 is the strongest alternative if the desired personality is more dreamlike; C12 is the quieter editorial choice.

All three are materially less cluttered than C9/C10. I would still simplify some foreground grass and rock hatching: the lower edge remains busy around the helper and pause control. That is a refinement, not a reason to discard the direction. These are aesthetic judgments from inspecting the actual images, not usability or performance measurements.
